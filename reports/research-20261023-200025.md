# KairoFX strategy research - 2026-10-23 20:00 UTC

Out-of-sample walk-forward test of whether any strategy has a positive after-cost edge. A negative result is a valid result.

## Setup

- Pairs: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, AUDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, NZDUSD, EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY; H1 bars from Yahoo Finance (mid prices, ~2.8 years: Jan 2024 - Oct 2026).
- Split per pair by bar count: TRAIN first 50%, VALIDATE next 25%, TEST final 25% (trades assigned by entry time).
- Selection: best TRAIN expectancy aggregated over pairs, min 60 trades. Selection function only receives train trades. VALIDATE/TEST never used for selection.
- Costs: per-pair spread charged round-trip + 0.2 pip slippage on stop exits; entry at next bar open; stop assumed first when SL and TP are both inside one bar.
- **Multiple-testing disclosure: 27 configs tried in total** (baseline_pattern_rsi: 1, trend_pullback: 8, donchian_breakout: 6, asian_range_breakout: 4, rsi2_trend: 8). The selected config is the best of its grid on train, so even train expectancy is optimistic.
- Verdict rule: 'candidate edge' only if net expectancy > 0 on BOTH validate and test, >= 100 combined validate+test trades, AND bootstrap 95% CI lower bound of combined validate+test expectancy >= 0. Otherwise 'no demonstrated edge'.

## Caveats

- Yahoo FX data are indicative mid prices with no bid/ask; spreads are assumptions and real spreads widen at rollover, news and illiquid hours.
- Only ~2 years of H1 history: one or two market regimes. Each split holds ~6 months per pair.
- Pairs are correlated (USD legs), so trades across pairs are not independent; the bootstrap CI treats them as independent and is therefore too narrow.
- Trades are assigned to splits by entry time; a trade entered late in one split may exit in the next.
- Results with CIs spanning zero cannot distinguish a small edge from noise.
