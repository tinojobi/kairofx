# Multi-market long-horizon trend/momentum: walk-forward report

## Verdict: no demonstrated edge

Rule: a strategy is a 'candidate edge' only if net expectancy R > 0 AND portfolio Sharpe > 0.3 on BOTH validate and test, >= 150 combined validate+test trades, and the monthly-block-bootstrap 95% CI lower bound on combined validate+test expectancy R is > 0. Otherwise 'no demonstrated edge'.

| tsmom | tsmom_L250 | no demonstrated edge |

## Protocol

- Granularity D; data 2004-12-31 to 2026-10-22; instruments used: 22 (AUD_CAD AUD_JPY AUD_NZD AUD_USD CAD_JPY CHF_JPY EUR_AUD EUR_CAD EUR_CHF EUR_GBP EUR_JPY EUR_USD GBP_AUD GBP_CAD GBP_CHF GBP_JPY GBP_USD NZD_JPY NZD_USD USD_CAD USD_CHF USD_JPY).
- TRAIN: start..2014-12-31; VALIDATE: 2015-01-01..2019-12-31; TEST: 2020-01-01..end. Trades are assigned by EXIT date.
- Signals at bar close, entry at next bar open, causal indicators, long and short, one position per instrument.
- Cost: trailing 250-bar median (ask_open-bid_open) at entry per round trip, plus 0.5x spread extra on stop exits. Gaps through a stop fill at the open.
- Sizing: 0.25% of equity risked per position at inception (stop distance 3 or 4 ATR(20); 3 ATR for sizing where the strategy has no hard stop). pnl% = R x risk%. Caps: 12 total, 4 per asset-class group.
- Equity: closed-trade equity, realised at exit date, compounded; daily business-day returns. Not marked to market, so drawdown and vol are understated; 'stressed maxDD' additionally charges each trade's worst adverse excursion (ignores stacking of concurrent trades).
- Selection: per strategy, highest TRAIN net expectancy R with >= 80 TRAIN trades. The selection function receives TRAIN trades only.
- **Configs tried: 13 total** (donchian 6, ema_cross 4, tsmom 3). All are listed in the appendix. Three strategy families were tried, so one 'candidate edge' among three carries a multiple-testing discount.

## Caveats

- Regime coverage: the global financial crisis (2008) sits only in TRAIN; VALIDATE and TEST contain no comparable equity/credit crash except the 2020 COVID shock (TEST). Trend following performance is dominated by a few episodes, so any split has few independent observations.
- Daily bars give a few hundred trades per config at most. Trades cluster in time and across correlated markets; the month-block bootstrap only partly reflects that.
- Universe is fixed by what OANDA lists today (survivorship in the instrument list); history starts at each instrument's export start; spreads in early years may be stale or missing.
- Trades assigned by exit date; a trade may be entered in one window and exit in the next. Trades blocked by caps are dropped, not queued. Same-timestamp cap conflicts resolve alphabetically by instrument.
- Non-USD P&L is return-based (R x risk%); no currency conversion and no financing/swap costs are modelled, which flatters carry-adverse positions.
- A negative result is a result: this harness does not tune after seeing validate/test.
